# Changelog

All notable changes to Pairwise are recorded here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Moving a window and its partner together while dragging, as pictures in one window of Pairwise's own.
- Live pictures while dragging, at a chosen frame rate.
- A stress test of dragging that the app runs on itself.
- Joining two windows with Command, or with a lock on their seam, and coming apart in the same ways.
- Resizing both windows of a pair at their seam, stopping where a window will go no further.
- Bringing both windows forward at a join, and a sound at a join and a parting, which a setting turns off.
- A choice between joining any window and only windows of the same app.
- An About item in the menu, with the version.
- The app icon.
