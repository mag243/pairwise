# Changelog

## [Unreleased]

- No gap between two windows after dragging their seam, when a window such as Terminal rounds its width to whole characters.

## [0.2.0]

- Pairwise needs only Accessibility now. While dragging you see an empty plane with each window's icon and title, and nothing is recorded.
- Still or live pictures while dragging, new settings that take Screen Recording. Live keeps video playing and the seam sharp, with macOS's purple recording icon.
- Settings comes to the front when you open it from the menu, also from another desktop.
- A page at https://mag243.github.io/pairwise/.

## [0.1.1]

Nothing new to see: this release tries out updating from 0.1.0.

## [0.1.0]

The first release.

- Join two windows of any app: drag one to the other and press Command, or click the open lock on the seam of two windows side by side.
- A pair moves together, shares its seam and comes to the front together.
- Part them with the closed lock, or by pressing Command while dragging one.
- Pairs last through a quit and a restart.
